# Learning preferences

- Lesson objectives determine the topic, scope, and assessment criteria.
- Use the selected folder and available resources as context.
- Calibrate difficulty through the learner's request and demonstrated work.
- Prefer small practical exercises in the existing module folders.
- Independent implementation; hints and full solutions only on request.
- Concise requirements and reviews in chat.
- No current-exercise documents, automatic review files, or progress logs.
- Adapt guidance and challenge without imposing a fixed curriculum.
